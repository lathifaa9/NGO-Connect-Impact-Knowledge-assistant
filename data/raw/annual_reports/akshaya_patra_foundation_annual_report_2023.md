## 2. Flagship Program: PM POSHAN (Mid-Day Meal Scheme)

Akshaya Patra is the world's largest non-governmental organization implementing the government-mandated **Pradhan Mantri Poshan Shakti Nirman (PM POSHAN)** scheme in partnership with the Ministry of Education and State Governments.

### Operational Infrastructure:
- **Centralized Mega Kitchens**: 67 state-of-the-art centralized mechanized kitchens capable of preparing up to 100,000 hot, nutritious meals per kitchen within 3 hours.
- **Strict Food Safety Standards**: Certified under ISO 22000:2018 (Food Safety Management System) ensuring zero physical contamination, balanced macronutrients, and fortified micronutrients.
- **Customized Thermal Delivery Fleets**: Custom insulated delivery vans transporting hot meals to remote primary and upper-primary government schools within a 50 km radius.

---

## 3. Key Quantitative Outcomes and Reported Impact (2023–24)

- **Daily Meals Served**: Over **2.2 million children** served hot, hygienic mid-day meals every single school day.
- **School Reach**: **24,100 government and government-aided schools** covered across 72 operational locations.
- **Total Annual Meals Distributed**: Exceeded **440 million cumulative school meals** in the 2023–2024 academic year.
- **Impact on School Retention**:
  - Independent social impact studies conducted by KPMG and Nielsen documented a **14.2% increase in elementary school student attendance**.
  - Documented a **19.8% increase in female student enrollment** in rural beneficiary schools.
  - Substantial decline in classroom hunger, leading to measurable gains in afternoon student attention span and academic comprehension.

## 4. Government Funding & Corporate CSR Collaboration

- **Public-Private Partnership (PPP)**: Funded through a structured tripartite model:
  1. Subsidized food grains (wheat and rice) and cooking cost reimbursement provided by the Central and State Governments under PM POSHAN norms.
  2. Kitchen capital infrastructure, delivery vehicles, and nutritional fortification funded via corporate CSR contributions.
- **CSR Grant Partners**: Infosys Foundation, Tata Motors, State Bank of India, Cisco Systems, HDFC Bank, and Larsen & Toubro.
