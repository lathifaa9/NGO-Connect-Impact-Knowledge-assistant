## 1. Statutory Mandate: Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013

Under Section 135 of the *Companies Act, 2013* and the *Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Rules, 2014* (amended in 2021 and 2022), India became the first country in the world to mandate statutory Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) spending for qualifying corporate entities.

### Applicability Thresholds:
Every company (including its holding or subsidiary, and foreign company branches in India) having:
1. **Net worth** of ₹500 crore or more, OR
2. **Turnover** of ₹1,000 crore or more, OR
3. **Net profit** of ₹5 crore or more  
during the immediately preceding financial year must constitute a **CSR Committee** of the Board and spend at least **2% of the average net profits** made during the three immediately preceding financial years on approved CSR initiatives.

## 2. Permissible Activities under Schedule VII

CSR expenditure must be directed strictly towards activities enumerated under **Schedule VII** of the Companies Act, 2013:

1. **Eradicating Hunger, Poverty & Malnutrition**: Promoting health care, sanitation, making available safe drinking water, and contributing to the Swachh Bharat Kosh.
2. **Promoting Education**: Special education and employment enhancing vocation skills, especially among children, women, elderly, and the differently abled, and livelihood enhancement projects.
3. **Promoting Gender Equality & Women Empowerment**: Setting up homes and hostels for women and orphans, old age homes, day care centres, and measures for reducing inequalities faced by socially and economically backward groups.
4. **Ensuring Environmental Sustainability**: Ecological balance, protection of flora and fauna, animal welfare, agroforestry, conservation of natural resources, and maintaining quality of soil, air, and water.
5. **Protection of National Heritage, Art & Culture**: Restoration of historical buildings and works of art, setting up public libraries, promotion and development of traditional arts and handicrafts.
6. **Measures for the Benefit of Armed Forces Veterans**: War widows and their dependents.
7. **Training to Promote Rural Sports**: Nationally recognized sports, paralympic sports, and Olympic sports.
8. **Contribution to Prime Minister’s National Relief Fund (PMNRF)** or Clean Ganga Fund or any other fund set up by the Central Government for socio-economic development and relief.
9. **Rural Development Projects & Slum Area Development**: Infrastructure, micro-irrigation, solar lighting in remote villages.
10. **Disaster Management**: Including relief, rehabilitation, and reconstruction activities.

## 3. Mandatory Registration of NGOs via Form CSR-1

Effective 1st April 2021, any NGO or implementing agency intending to undertake any CSR activity funded by a corporation must be registered with the Central Government by filing **Form CSR-1** electronically with the Registrar of Companies (ROC).

### Eligible Implementing Agencies:
- Section 8 Company, Registered Public Trust, or Registered Society established under Section 12A/12AB and 80G of the Income Tax Act with an established track record of at least **three years** in undertaking similar activities.
- Upon successful submission of Form CSR-1, the MCA portal automatically generates a unique **CSR Registration Number** (Format: `CSRXXXXXXXX`). Companies are prohibited from disbursing CSR funds to any NGO lacking an active CSR-1 registration number.

## 4. Impact Assessment Rules

Under the amended CSR Rules (Rule 8(3)):
- A company having an average CSR obligation of **₹10 crore or more** in the three immediately preceding financial years is legally required to undertake an independent **Impact Assessment** for each CSR project having an outlay of **₹1 crore or more**, which has been completed not less than one year before undertaking the study.
- The impact assessment must be conducted by an independent third-party agency (such as academic institutions, specialized evaluation research bodies, or certified evaluators).
- The impact assessment report must be placed before the Board and annexed to the annual report on CSR.
