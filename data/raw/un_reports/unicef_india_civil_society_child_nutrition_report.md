## 1. Executive Summary

This report evaluates the strategic impact of joint programming between **UNICEF India** and over 120 national and state-level **Civil Society Organizations (CSOs) / Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs)** in advancing child survival, infant nutrition, and Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) standards across high-burden districts.

---

## 2. Core Thematic Collaborations

### A. POSHAN Abhiyaan & First 1,000 Days Nutrition
- Strengthening the critical window from conception to a child's second birthday (First 1,000 Days).
- NGOs deployed community nutrition mobilizers to counsel pregnant women on exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months, complementary feeding practices, and consumption of Iron-Folic Acid (IFA) tablets.
- Supporting over **12,000 Anganwadi Workers (AWWs)** and **Accredited Social Health Activists (ASHAs)** with home-visit counseling tools and visual aids.

### B. Swachh Bharat Mission (Grameen) & Jal Jeevan Mission Support
- Civil society partners mobilized rural communities for open defecation free (ODF) sustainability and ODF-Plus verification (solid and liquid waste management).
- Training village Village Water and Sanitation Committees (VWSCs) to conduct periodic testing of drinking water sources using Field Test Kits (FTKs) for chemical and bacteriological contamination.

### C. Child Protection & Emergency Response
- Operating dedicated community toll-free counseling desks and establishing safe spaces for children during monsoon floods in Assam, Bihar, and Uttar Pradesh.
- Training community volunteers in preventing child sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA) and early identification of child trafficking threats.

---

## 3. Key Findings & Reported Outcomes

- **Reduction in Child Wasting**: Intervention districts with active NGO-community partnership showed a **4.2 percentage point greater reduction** in child wasting rates compared to non-partner control blocks over an 18-month cycle.
- **Handwashing with Soap**: Post-intervention school evaluations indicated that regular handwashing with soap before meals increased from 38% to **84%** across 4,200 rural primary schools.
- **Village Water Security**: Over 8,500 village drinking water sources tested and certified, preventing seasonal outbreaks of water-borne diarrheal diseases.
