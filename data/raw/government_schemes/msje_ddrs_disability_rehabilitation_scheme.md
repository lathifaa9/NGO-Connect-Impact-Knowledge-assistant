## 1. Scheme Overview and Objectives

The **Deendayal Disabled Rehabilitation Scheme (DDRS)** is an umbrella Central Sector Scheme administered by the **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MSJE)**. It operates predominantly through financial grant-in-aid disbursed to **Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) and Voluntary Organizations (VOs)** to facilitate equal opportunities, protection of rights, and full participation of Persons with Disabilities (PwDs) in society.

### Core Objectives:
1. Promote voluntary action in the disability sector and encourage civil society initiatives.
2. Ensure social justice, dignity, and educational/vocational rehabilitation for children and adults with intellectual, visual, hearing, locomotor, and multiple disabilities.
3. Provide financial assistance to non-profit organizations for establishing and running special schools, early intervention centres, and vocational training facilities.

---

## 2. Eligibility Criteria for NGOs under DDRS

To be eligible for grant-in-aid under DDRS, an NGO must satisfy the following conditions:
1. **Legal Registration**: Registered under the *Societies Registration Act, 1860*, or the *Indian Trusts Act, 1882*, or *Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013* for at least **two years**.
2. **RPwD Act Registration**: Mandatory registration under Section 51 of the *Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016* with the competent State Disability Authority.
3. **NGO Darpan Unique ID**: Mandatory active registration on the NITI Aayog NGO Darpan portal.
4. **Demonstrated Track Record**: Sound financial standing and active management of disability-related social rehabilitation services.
5. **Proper Management Committee**: A secular, non-discriminatory governing body with transparent audit reports.

---

## 3. Permissible Project Profiles Supported under DDRS

The scheme funds 9 standard model projects executed through registered NGOs:
1. **Special Schools for Children with Intellectual Disabilities**
2. **Special Schools for Children with Hearing and Speech Impairment**
3. **Special Schools for Children with Visual Impairment**
4. **Preschool and Early Intervention & Training Centers (ages 0 to 6)**
5. **Vocational Training Centres (VTCs)** for adult persons with disabilities
6. **Community Based Rehabilitation (CBR) Projects** in rural and tribal districts
7. **Half-Way Homes for Psychosocial Rehabilitation of Treated and Controlled Mentally Ill Persons**
8. **District Disability Rehabilitation Centres (DDRCs)** operated by voluntary agencies
9. **Projects for Children with Cerebral Palsy and Multiple Disabilities**

---

## 4. Financial Norms & Quantum of Assistance

- **Cost Sharing**: The Central Government provides grant-in-aid up to **90% of the admissible recurring cost** (honorarium of special educators, psychologists, therapists, hostel food charges, assistive learning material) and non-recurring cost. In urban special areas and North-Eastern States, the grant extends up to **95%**.
- **Honorarium Scales**: Standardized monthly honorarium rates prescribed for qualified staff (e.g. Special Educators, Speech Therapists, Physiotherapists, Vocational Instructors).
- **Online Application Portal**: All grant applications, quarterly utilization certificates (UC), inspection reports by State Government officials, and audited accounts must be processed transparently via the **e-Anudaan** portal (`https://grants-msje.gov.in`).
