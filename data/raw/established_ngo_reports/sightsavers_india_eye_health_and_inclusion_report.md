## 2. Core Program Pillars & Activities

### A. Netra Vasant (Rural Eye Health)
- Partnering with district hospital eye units and charitable mission eye hospitals.
- Upgrading vision centres in rural blocks with slit lamps, auto-refractometers, and tonometers.
- Training primary community health workers (ASHAs) in basic vision screening and referral.

### B. RAAHI (National Truckers Eye Health Programme)
- Setting up static and mobile vision screening booths at major freight transport hubs, highway toll plazas, and trans-shipment container terminals across National Highways.
- Providing on-spot eye examinations, refraction testing, and customized corrective spectacles to long-haul truck drivers.

### C. Inclusive Education for Children with Visual Impairment
- Providing braille kits, assistive screen-reader laptops, Daisy players, and low-vision devices to visually impaired students attending mainstream government schools.
- Training resource teachers in inclusive classroom pedagogy.

## 3. Key Quantitative Outcomes and Reported Impact (2022–23)

- **Eye Examinations Conducted**: Over **1,850,000 people screened** for visual impairment across rural communities.
- **Sight-Saving Surgeries**: Supported **128,000 cataract surgeries**, restoring functional eyesight to disadvantaged rural patients.
- **Spectacles Provided**: Distributed **310,000 custom prescription eyeglasses**, correcting unaddressed refractive errors.
- **Truck Drivers Screened (RAAHI)**:
  - Screened **450,000 commercial truck drivers** across 28 highway transport hubs.
  - 43% of tested drivers were identified with uncorrected refractive vision defects posing severe road safety hazards; 91% were fitted with free corrective eyeglasses on-site.
- **Students Supported in Inclusive Education**: Supported **14,200 visually impaired children** in 3,200 mainstream government schools, ensuring zero school drop-out.
