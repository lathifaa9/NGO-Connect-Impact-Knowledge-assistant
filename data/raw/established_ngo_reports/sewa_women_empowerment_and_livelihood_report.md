---

## 2. Strategic Pillars & Programs

### A. Cooperative Banking & Financial Inclusion (SEWA Bank Model)
- Providing specialized, collateral-free micro-credit, recurring savings accounts, and financial literacy training tailored to non-literate women.
- Eliminating reliance on predatory informal moneylenders charging up to 120% annual interest.

### B. Social Security & Community Healthcare (Lok Swasthya SEWA)
- Running community-owned primary healthcare cooperatives, low-cost generic pharmacy distribution, and micro-insurance coverage (*VimoSEWA*) protecting against catastrophic health expenditure, loss of work tools, and accidental disability.

### C. Skill Upgradation & Market Linkages (SEWA Trade Facilitation Centre - STFC)
- Training women artisans in contemporary design, quality testing, and digital e-commerce order fulfillment for traditional embroidery (Kutch, Chikan, Phulkari).
- Assisting home-based food producers with Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) licensing and collective organic procurement.

---

## 3. Key Quantitative Outcomes and Reported Impact (2022–23)

- **Active Membership Base**: Over **2,100,000 self-employed women** organized across 18 Indian states.
- **Financial Inclusion Milestones**:
  - Mobilized cumulative micro-savings exceeding **₹420 crore** across cooperative banking units.
  - Disbursed **₹285 crore in micro-loans** for purchasing productive tools (sewing machines, hand-carts, cattle, solar dryers) with a repayment recovery rate of **97.8%**.
- **Income Enhancement**: Women members recorded an average **38% increase in net household income** within 24 months of joining SEWA enterprise cooperatives.
- **Healthcare & Nutrition Reach**: Delivered preventive healthcare education and non-communicable disease screening to **380,000 women and children** through 1,200 trained community health workers (*Barefoot Health Workers*).
