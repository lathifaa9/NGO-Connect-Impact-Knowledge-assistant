## 1. Overview of NGO-Darpan Portal

The **NGO-Darpan** (formerly NGO Partnership System) is a flagship digital portal maintained by **NITI Aayog** in collaboration with the **National Informatics Centre (NIC)**. It serves as the primary interface between Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) / Voluntary Organizations (VOs) and Central Ministries, Departments, and Government Bodies in India.

The portal provides an electronic database of VOs/NGOs and generates a system-generated **Unique Identity Number (NGO Darpan Unique ID)**. Registration on the NGO Darpan portal is a mandatory prerequisite for any NGO seeking grants, project sanctions, or funding from Ministries and Departments of the Government of India, as well as for obtaining registration under the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act (FCRA) administered by the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA).

### Primary Objectives of NGO Darpan
1. **Single-Window Registration**: Create a transparent, verifiable centralized repository of all voluntary organizations operating across Indian States and Union Territories.
2. **Grant-in-Aid Facilitation**: Track government grant allocations, monitor scheme execution, and minimize duplicate funding across Ministries.
3. **Public Accountability**: Allow citizens, government agencies, and donors to look up genuine NGOs, verify their operating sectors, key office bearers, and past project performance.
4. **Inter-Ministerial Linkage**: Integrate directly with Ministry portals including Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Ministry of Women and Child Development, Ministry of Culture, and MHA.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria for NGO Darpan Registration

To register on NGO Darpan, an organization must be a non-profit voluntary entity legally established under one of the recognized statutory frameworks in India:
1. **Societies**: Registered under the *Societies Registration Act, 1860* (or State-specific Societies Registration Acts).
2. **Public Trusts**: Registered under the *Indian Trusts Act, 1882* or Bombay Public Trusts Act, 1950 (or relevant state charitable trust law).
3. **Section 8 Companies**: Incorporated under *Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013* (or Section 25 of the Companies Act, 1956).

*Note: Proprietary firms, partnerships, unregistered clubs, and commercial business entities are strictly ineligible.*

## 3. Mandatory Documents and Information Required

Before initiating the online registration process, the organization must have the following documents and details ready in digital format:
1. **Registration Certificate**: Scanned copy of the legal registration certificate issued by the Registrar of Societies, Charity Commissioner, or Registrar of Companies (ROC) (PDF format, max 2MB).
2. **PAN Card of the NGO**: Permanent Account Number issued by the Income Tax Department in the name of the entity.
3. **PAN and Aadhaar of Office Bearers**: Minimum of 3 governing body members/trustees/directors (President/Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer) must provide verified PAN and Aadhaar details with OTP verification.
4. **FCRA Details (if applicable)**: FCRA Registration Number, date of grant, validity status, and dedicated SBI New Delhi Main Branch account details.
5. **Income Tax Registrations**: Details of Section 12A / 12AB and Section 80G registrations under the Income Tax Act, 1961.
6. **Past Experience & Key Focus Areas**: Operational sectors (e.g., Education, Health, Rural Development), major activities conducted over the past 3 years, and details of any government grants received.

## 4. Step-by-Step Registration Process

1. **Sign Up**: Visit `https://ngodarpan.gov.in` and click on "Sign Up". Provide the official email address and authorized mobile number.
2. **Entity Details Submission**: Enter legal name of VO/NGO, registration number, date of registration, registered address, and state/district of operation.
3. **Member Authentication**: Enter details of all governing body members. Aadhaar e-KYC or PAN validation is performed in real-time against central government databases.
4. **Document Upload**: Upload the copy of the registration certificate and PAN card.
5. **Generation of Unique ID**: Upon verification of submitted documents by NITI Aayog administrators, an official Unique ID is issued in the format: `XX/YYYY/NNNNNNN` (where XX is the State code, YYYY is the year of registration, and NNNNNNN is the serial sequence).

---

## 5. How to Search and Verify an NGO on Darpan

Citizens, corporate donors, and government evaluators can search for registered NGOs through the public directory:
- **Search by State/District**: View all registered voluntary organizations working in a specific geographic administrative area.
- **Search by Sector**: Filter NGOs working in thematic sectors such as *Children, Education, Environment, Health & Family Welfare, Women Development, Rural Development, Differently Abled*.
- **Search by Unique ID or Name**: Directly verify legal status, operational history, blacklisted status (if any), and valid contact credentials.

---

## 6. Compliance and Renewal Obligations

- **Profile Updating**: NGOs must update their profile details annually, including changes in governing body members, financial statements, and ongoing projects.
- **Blacklisting Safeguards**: In case of non-compliance, financial embezzlement, or submission of forged certificates, NITI Aayog marks the organization as *Blacklisted*, which immediately disqualifies it from all Central and State government grants and FCRA permissions.
