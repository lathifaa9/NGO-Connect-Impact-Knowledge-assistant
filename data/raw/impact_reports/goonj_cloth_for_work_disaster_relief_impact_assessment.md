## 2. Core Initiative: "Cloth for Work" (CFW) / Dignity for Work (DFW)

### Methodology & Philosophy:
In traditional relief and charity paradigms, goods are handed out as passive charity. Under Goonj’s **Cloth for Work** model, rural community members identify local pressing infrastructural and developmental issues (e.g. repairing a broken bridge, cleaning a choked village pond, digging an irrigation canal, laying a road).

The villagers contribute collective voluntary labor (*Shramdaan*) to resolve the problem. In return, Goonj rewards participating households with customized **"Family Kits"** containing clothing, winter woolens, kitchen utensils, school bags, and hygiene essentials collected from urban donation networks.

---

## 3. Thematic Programs & Initiatives

1. **Rahat (Disaster Relief & Long-Term Rehabilitation)**: Rapid response during floods, cyclones, landslides, and earthquakes, transitioning smoothly from emergency dry rations into long-term infrastructure reconstruction.
2. **Not Just a Piece of Cloth (NJPC)**: Addressing the severe taboo around menstrual health in rural India. Goonj manufactures and distributes **"My Pad"** – clean, hygienic, biodegradable, 100% cotton cloth sanitary napkins made from recycled urban cotton fabric.
3. **School to School (S2S)**: Upcycling urban surplus school bags, stationery, water bottles, and footwear into comprehensive school kits distributed to children in neglected rural village schools.

## 4. Quantified Impact Indicators & Findings (2022–23)

- **Material Channeling**: Processed and distributed over **6,200 metric tons** of urban surplus material.
- **Rural Infrastructure Activities Completed through Shramdaan**:
  - Over **4,200 village-level development activities** executed by rural communities.
  - **480 kilometers** of rural unpaved roads and pathways cleared and repaired.
  - **320 village ponds and water reservoirs** desilted and restored, replenishing groundwater tables.
  - **110 bamboo foot-bridges** constructed across flood-prone waterways in Assam, Bihar, and West Bengal, connecting isolated villages to primary schools and health clinics.
- **Menstrual Hygiene Reach (NJPC)**:
  - Over **4.5 million clean cloth pads ("My Pad")** produced and distributed.
  - Conducted **18,000 village "Chuppi Todo" (Break the Silence)** menstrual hygiene awareness workshops, dispelling harmful taboos and significantly reducing reproductive tract infections (RTI) among rural women.
- **School Kits Distributed**: Over **280,000 rural children** received comprehensive learning kits, directly curbing elementary school absenteeism during monsoon seasons.
