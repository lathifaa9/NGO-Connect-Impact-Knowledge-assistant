## 1. Context of DAY-NRLM and Civil Society Collaboration

The **Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM)**, launched by the **Ministry of Rural Development (MoRD)**, is one of the world's largest community mobilization programs targeting rural poverty. It seeks to mobilize rural poor women into self-managed **Self-Help Groups (SHGs)** and federated community institutions (Village Organizations and Cluster Level Federations).

Recognizing that specialized Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) and Civil Society Organizations (CSOs) possess grassroots trust, pedagogical innovations, and specialized technical expertise, DAY-NRLM engages NGOs as **National and State Resource Organizations (NROs / SROs)** and **Project Implementation Agencies (PIAs)**.

---

## 2. Key Priority Arenas for NGO Partnership

DAY-NRLM collaborates with qualified NGOs in several specialized developmental verticals:

1. **Reaching the Ultra-Poor and Vulnerable**: Mobilizing Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs), single women, manual scavengers, and households affected by severe disability through graduation model approaches.
2. **Promotion of Non-Timber Forest Produce (NTFP) and Organic Agro-Ecology**: Providing sustainable farming training, climate-resilient agriculture, soil health improvement, and bio-fertilizer production.
3. **Farm and Non-Farm Value Chain Development**: Forming **Producer Groups (PGs)** and **Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)**, facilitating market linkages, cold-chain logistics, and digital buyer onboarding (e.g. GeM portal, ONDC).
4. **Artisan & Rural Crafts Promotion**: Enhancing design quality, branding, and e-commerce marketing for traditional handloom and handicraft clusters.
5. **Mahila Kisan Sashaktikaran Pariyojana (MKSP)**: Empowering women smallholder farmers with regenerative agricultural skills and drudgery-reducing tools.

---

## 3. Eligibility Criteria for NGO Selection under DAY-NRLM

Organizations applying for partnership under DAY-NRLM must meet strict quality parameters:
- Minimum **5 years of operational experience** in grassroots community mobilization and rural livelihood enhancement.
- Active registration on **NITI Aayog NGO Darpan** with a clean regulatory track record.
- Audited financial statements for the past 3 consecutive years with average annual expenditure exceeding ₹50 lakh.
- Proven technical competency in running community-led microfinance, agro-value chains, or enterprise incubation.
- Presence of experienced, field-based multidisciplinary professionals in agriculture, social work, rural management, and animal husbandry.
