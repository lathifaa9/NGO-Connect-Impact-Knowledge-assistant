## 2. Mission and Core Objectives

Pratham is dedicated to ensuring that "Every child in school is learning well." As one of India's largest non-governmental organizations in the primary education sector, Pratham addresses the foundational learning crisis through evidence-based pedagogical innovations, community participation, and high-impact partnership with state governments.

---

## 3. Major Programs and Operational Activities

### A. Read India & "Teaching at the Right Level" (TaRL)
- **Core Activity**: Implementing remedial learning camps where children in Grades 3 to 5 are grouped according to their actual learning levels (beginner, letter, word, paragraph, story) rather than chronological age or grade.
- **Pedagogy**: Multi-graded activities, games, and engaging storybooks designed to bring children to basic literacy and numeracy within 30 to 50 days of intensive instruction.

### B. "Hamara Gaon" (Community-Based Learning Model)
- Active across 1,850 rural clusters and urban informal settlements.
- Training youth volunteers ("Mothers' Groups" and "Smart Saathis") to support daily learning at home and community libraries.

### C. "Second Chance" Program for Drop-out Girls and Young Women
- Focuses on adolescent girls and young women who were forced to discontinue formal schooling due to early marriage, financial constraints, or domestic burdens.
- Provides academic bridge coaching, digital literacy, and examination registration support to help girls complete their Grade 10 secondary school certifications through the National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) or State Open Boards.

### D. Pratham Vocational Training & Skilling
- Centers across 16 states training youth from low-income families in hospitality, electrical installation, automotive mechanics, healthcare assistance, and beauty care.

## 4. Key Quantitative Outcomes and Reported Impact (2023–24)

- **Total Direct Beneficiaries**: 5,230,000 children and youth supported across rural and urban centers.
- **Learning Gains**: Independent pre-and-post camp assessments indicated a **24.6% net increase** in children able to read grade-level texts fluently, and a **28.2% increase** in children capable of solving basic subtraction/division operations.
- **Second Chance Beneficiaries**: 48,200 young women enrolled; over 88% successfully passed their Grade 10 board examinations.
- **Youth Placements**: 32,400 youth trained in certified vocational skills, achieving a verified post-training employment placement rate of 74%.
- **Community Engagement**: Over 140,000 mothers mobilized in 18,000 village-level mothers' learning groups.

## 5. Partnerships with Government Schemes and CSR

- **Government Collaboration**: Formal technical MoU with 14 State Governments to support pedagogical roll-out of the Ministry of Education's **NIPUN Bharat Mission** (National Initiative for Proficiency in Reading with Understanding and Numeracy).
- **CSR Partners**: Long-term CSR grant partnerships with Tata Trusts, ICICI Foundation, L&T, HDFC Bank Parivartan, and Infosys Foundation.
