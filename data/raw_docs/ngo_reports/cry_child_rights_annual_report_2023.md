## 2. Strategic Focus Pillars

CRY works holistically across four fundamental child rights derived from the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (UNCRC):
1. **Right to Survival**: Child nutrition, immunization, maternal health, and combating infant mortality.
2. **Right to Development**: Access to quality, inclusive schooling under the Right to Education (RTE) Act.
3. **Right to Protection**: Eradicating child labor, preventing child marriage, ending child trafficking, and providing legal defense against abuse.
4. **Right to Participation**: Establishing Children's Collectives (Bal Panchayats) where children express their views on community safety and education.

## 3. Major Programs and Operational Activities

### A. Community Malnutrition Interventions
- Active across 3,400 village habitations and slum settlements.
- Regular anthropometric tracking (Height-for-Age, Weight-for-Age) of children aged 0–5 years to identify Severely Acute Malnourished (SAM) and Moderately Acute Malnourished (MAM) cases.
- Direct referral and tracking at government Malnutrition Treatment Centres (MTC) and revitalization of local Anganwadi Centres (AWCs).

### B. School Retention & RTE Enforcement
- Door-to-door enrollment drives targeting children of seasonal migrant laborers.
- Strengthening School Management Committees (SMCs) to demand proper sanitation facilities, especially clean, functional toilets for adolescent girls.

### C. Prevention of Child Marriage and Child Labor
- Operating community youth alert groups and adolescent girls' collectives.
- Direct collaboration with Village Child Protection Committees (VCPCs), Child Welfare Committees (CWCs), and Special Juvenile Police Units (SJPU).

## 4. Key Quantitative Outcomes and Reported Impact (2023–24)

- **Total Direct Beneficiaries**: 985,000 children across 3,650 project villages and slum settlements.
- **Child Protection Milestones**: Prevented **2,840 verified instances of child marriages** through timely community intervention and statutory reporting to local Child Marriage Prohibition Officers (CMPO).
- **Child Labor Rescues & Rehabilitation**: 3,120 children rescued from hazardous work and reintegrated into formal government schooling.
- **Nutrition Gains**: 89% of children aged 0–5 in CRY operational project areas were fully immunized; 18,400 malnourished children rehabilitated to normal health parameters.
- **Education Continuity**: 94% of children in operational project areas successfully transitioned from primary to upper primary school without dropping out.
