## 1. How Can Companies Implement CSR Through NGOs?

Under Rule 4(1) of the Companies (CSR Policy) Rules, 2014, a company may execute its CSR programmes either:
1. Directly through its own in-house CSR committee and field teams, OR
2. Through a registered entity established by the company itself (e.g. Reliance Foundation, Infosys Foundation, Tata Trusts), OR
3. Through an independent Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) registered as a Society, Trust, or Section 8 Company that holds:
   - Form CSR-1 Registration with MCA.
   - Section 12A/12AB & 80G tax registrations.
   - Minimum 3 years of demonstrated operational track record in similar social programmes.

## 2. Permissible vs. Non-Permissible CSR Expenses

### Permissible Expenses:
- Capital expenditure on building community assets (e.g., constructing rural health sub-centres, school sanitation units, community rainwater harvesting structures) provided ownership vests in the public or the Section 8 / Trust beneficiary community.
- Direct programmatic expenses: medicines, nutritional supplements, books, teacher stipends, solar installations.
- Administrative overheads directly attributable to CSR management, capped at **5% of total CSR expenditure** for the financial year.
- Impact assessment expenses: Up to **2% of total CSR expenditure** or **₹50 lakh**, whichever is less.

### Strictly Non-Permissible CSR Activities:
1. Activities undertaken in the normal course of business (commercial operations).
2. Projects benefiting exclusively the employees of the company or their families.
3. Political party donations or contributions under Section 182.
4. Activities carried out outside India (except for training of Indian sports personnel outside India).
5. Sponsorship of events or television programmes aimed at marketing products or services.
6. Activities fulfilling statutory legal requirements under other existing labour or safety laws.

## 3. Treatment of Unspent CSR Amounts (Section 135(5) & 135(6))

- **Ongoing Projects**: Any unspent CSR amount relating to an approved multi-year "Ongoing Project" must be transferred within 30 days from the end of the financial year to a special bank account opened in a scheduled bank called the **"Unspent CSR Account"**, and spent within three financial years.
- **Other Unspent Amounts**: If not related to an ongoing project, unspent funds must be transferred within six months of the financial year end to a Fund specified in Schedule VII (such as PM National Relief Fund, PM CARES, Clean Ganga Fund).
