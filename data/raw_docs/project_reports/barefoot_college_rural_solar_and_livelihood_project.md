## 2. Project Objectives

1. **Demystify Technology**: Train non-literate and semi-literate rural women (affectionately termed **"Solar Mamas"**) as competent solar engineers capable of installing, maintaining, and repairing decentralized solar home lighting systems.
2. **Environmental Decarbonization**: Replace hazardous and polluting kerosene lamps with clean, renewable off-grid solar electricity in remote tribal and desert hamlets.
3. **Water Security**: Construct underground rainwater harvesting structures in rural community schools and health centres.

---

## 3. Project Activities and Training Methodology

- **6-Month Solar Engineering Fellowship**: Practical hands-on training using visual color-coded manuals, diagrams, and physical assembly boards rather than written text, overcoming language and literacy barriers.
- **Solar Workshop Production**: Fabricating solar lanterns, charge controllers, integrated home lighting units, and solar parabolic cookers.
- **Village Energy Committees (VECs)**: Establishing democratic village bodies that determine monthly user tariffs for solar maintenance, fostering community financial autonomy.
- **Traditional Artisan Collectives**: Supporting rural women artisans through the *Barefoot Bazaars* cooperative producing recycled textile crafts, handlooms, and organic dry-land agro-products.

---

## 4. Key Quantitative Outcomes and Reported Impact

- **Solar Electrification**: 
  - **14,500 rural households** electrified with decentralized off-grid solar energy across 210 off-grid villages in Rajasthan, Ladakh, Bihar, and Jharkhand.
  - **Over 420 rural women ("Solar Mamas")** certified as master solar technicians.
  - Elimination of **180,000 liters of kerosene consumption annually**, preventing 450 metric tons of carbon emissions.
- **Rainwater Harvesting**:
  - Over **60 million liters of rainwater** harvested and stored across 450 village school cisterns, securing potable drinking water for 120,000 children throughout dry desert summers.
- **Livelihood Generation**:
  - Direct financial income disbursed to 1,250 artisan families, increasing average monthly household disposable income by 45%.
