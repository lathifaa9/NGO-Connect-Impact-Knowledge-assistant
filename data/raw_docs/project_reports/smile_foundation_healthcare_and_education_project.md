## 2. Project Objectives

1. **Smile on Wheels**: Provide doorstep preventive, promotive, and curative primary healthcare services to underserved urban slums and isolated rural habitations lacking primary health centres (PHCs).
2. **Mission Education**: Provide holistic education support, school retention mentoring, nutritional support, and digital learning infrastructure for children from disadvantaged socio-economic backgrounds.

---

## 3. Project Activities and Implementation Methodology

### A. Smile on Wheels (Mobile Healthcare Units)
- Deploying fully-equipped mobile medical vans staffed by qualified doctors, nurses, pharmacists, and lab technicians.
- Providing free outpatient (OPD) consultations, point-of-care diagnostic tests (blood glucose, hemoglobin, urine routine, ECG, malaria, typhoid), and distribution of essential generic medicines.
- Running specialized antenatal (ANC) and postnatal care (PNC) camps for expectant and nursing mothers.

### B. Mission Education Centers
- Operating non-formal education and remedial learning support centers co-located within community municipal schools and slum clusters.
- Providing mid-day nutritious snacks, textbooks, school uniforms, and digital tablets.
- Conducting regular parent-teacher counseling sessions to reduce student absenteeism and curb child labor.

---

## 4. Target Beneficiaries & Locations

- **Geographic Locations**: Slum clusters of Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, plus remote rural blocks in Odisha, Rajasthan, and Madhya Pradesh.
- **Target Groups**: Informal settlement residents, daily wage migrant workers, women, adolescent girls, and out-of-school children.

---

## 5. Reported Outcomes and Quantified Impact

- **Healthcare Reach**: 
  - **1,420,000 patients** treated through 84 mobile medical units across 420 urban slums and rural villages.
  - **72% of healthcare beneficiaries were women and children**.
  - **185,000 diagnostic laboratory tests** conducted free of charge.
  - **42,000 pregnant women** received comprehensive antenatal counseling and nutritional supplements.
- **Education Reach**:
  - **48,500 children** enrolled in 240 Mission Education centers across 23 states.
  - **51% of enrolled children were girls**.
  - **92% overall retention rate** into mainstream government formal schools.
  - Demonstrated a 31% improvement in English and Mathematics learning outcomes compared to baseline scores.
