## 1. Annual Return Compliance (Form FC-4)

Every organization registered under the FCRA or holding Prior Permission must file an online **Annual Return in Form FC-4** for every financial year (1st April to 31st March).

### Key Filing Rules:
- **Mandatory Deadline**: Form FC-4 must be submitted within nine months of the closure of the financial year, i.e., on or before **31st December**.
- **Nil Returns Mandatory**: Even if an association has received zero foreign contributions and incurred zero foreign expenditure during a financial year, submitting a **"Nil Return"** is legally compulsory.
- **Accompanying Documents**:
  1. Audited statement of accounts (Receipt and Payment Account, Income and Expenditure Statement, Balance Sheet) certified by an independent Chartered Accountant.
  2. Certificate from the Chartered Accountant in **Form FC-4 Annexure**.
  3. Certified bank statements of the designated SBI New Delhi Main Branch account and all linked utilization accounts.

## 2. Permitted and Prohibited Sources of Foreign Funding

### Permitted Foreign Contributions:
- Donations, delivery, or transfer made by foreign governments, foreign public charities, multinational philanthropic foundations (e.g. Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, Ford Foundation), multilateral UN agencies, and foreign individuals.

### Prohibited Recipients (Section 3):
The following persons/entities are strictly prohibited from receiving foreign contributions:
1. Candidates for election.
2. Journalists, columnists, cartoonists, editors, owners, printers, or publishers of registered newspapers.
3. Judges, government servants, or employees of any corporation owned or controlled by the government.
4. Members of any Legislature (MPs, MLAs, MLCs).
5. Political parties or their office bearers, and organizations of a political nature.

## 3. Quarterly Reporting of Foreign Receipts

Under Rule 13 of the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Rules, 2011, every registered association must publish on its official public website (or on the MHA online portal) the details of foreign contributions received within **45 days** of the end of each calendar quarter, including:
- Name of the foreign donor.
- Country of origin.
- Date of receipt.
- Exact monetary amount received in foreign currency and INR equivalent.
- Programmatic purpose of contribution.
