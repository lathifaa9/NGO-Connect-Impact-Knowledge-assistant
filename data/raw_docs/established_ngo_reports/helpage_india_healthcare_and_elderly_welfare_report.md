## 2. Core Healthcare Programs & Interventions

### A. Mobile Healthcare Units (MHU)
- HelpAge operates India's largest non-governmental network of **Mobile Healthcare Units (MHUs)**.
- Medical vans reach rural villages, tribal habitations, and urban slums on fixed weekly schedules.
- Vans are equipped with doctors, pharmacists, basic diagnostic equipment (blood pressure, blood glucose, ECG), and provide free essential medicines for chronic age-related ailments (hypertension, diabetes, arthritis, bronchial asthma).

### B. Restoration of Vision (Cataract Surgeries)
- Conducting free screening camps for age-related cataract blindness in partnership with regional eye hospitals and district health societies under the National Programme for Control of Blindness and Visual Impairment (NPCBVI).
- Sponsoring free Intraocular Lens (IOL) micro-surgeries, post-operative care, dark glasses, and follow-up examinations.

### C. Palliative Home Care & Geriatric Care Centres
- Providing specialized palliative pain management, bed-sore dressing, and psychological support for terminally ill, bedridden senior citizens in their homes.
- Operating model Old Age Homes (OAHs) and Day Care Centres providing shelter, nutrition, and companionship.

## 3. Advocacy and Elder Helpline (14567)

- Managing national elder abuse prevention campaigns, legal aid clinics, and operating the National Elderline in collaboration with the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
- Conducting national research surveys on the state of elderly care and digital financial inclusion in India.

## 4. Key Quantitative Outcomes and Reported Impact (2023–24)

- **Mobile Healthcare Treatments**: Over **3,150,000 treatments** delivered to disadvantaged senior citizens across 2,400 village and urban slum sites.
- **Cataract Surgeries Performed**: Successfully supported **42,500 sight-restoring cataract surgeries** across rural districts, with a verified success rate of 99.2%.
- **Elder Self-Help Groups (ESHGs)**: Supported **8,400 Elder Self-Help Groups** encompassing 115,000 older persons, creating collective savings of over ₹28 crore used for emergency medical loans and small livelihood activities (goat rearing, spice processing, tailoring).
- **Elderline Grievance Redressal**: Responded to **180,000 calls** on Elderline (14567), rescuing 1,420 abandoned senior citizens and facilitating legal reunion or admission into recognized care shelters.
