## 2. Priority Research & Policy Programs

### A. Clean Air & Sustainable Urban Mobility
- Real-time air quality monitoring of particulate matter (PM2.5 and PM10) across northern and central Indian industrial cities.
- Technical policy advocacy for the transition of public transportation fleets to Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) and electric mobility (EV).
- Action plans for curbing crop residue burning (stubble burning) through in-situ bio-decomposer technologies and ex-situ biomass pelletization.

### B. Water Management & Rainwater Harvesting (Jal Swaraj)
- Promoting decentralised rooftop rainwater harvesting and urban catchment revival.
- Technical research into traditional water harvesting structures (Kunds, Bawdis, Johads, Tankas) across arid zones.
- Establishing the "Ganga River Basin Management" and fecal sludge management frameworks for Tier 2 and Tier 3 riverine cities.

### C. Renewable Energy & Decarbonization
- Tracking renewable energy integration, solar mini-grids for rural micro-enterprises, and industrial decarbonization in heavy steel and cement clusters.
- Public policy advocacy at international climate summits (UNFCCC COP) demanding climate finance equity and carbon budget allocations for developing nations.

## 3. Key Quantitative Outcomes and Reported Impact (2022–23)

- **Environmental Capacity Building**: Trained over **4,800 municipal engineers, state pollution control board (SPCB) officials, and university researchers** in decentralized wastewater treatment and green building design.
- **Rooftop Rainwater Harvesting Designs**: Formulated detailed architectural engineering designs for **1,400 schools and public institutions**, harvesting an estimated 140 million liters of clean rainwater per monsoon cycle.
- **Air Quality Action Plans**: Formulated City Clean Air Action Plans under the National Clean Air Programme (NCAP) for **34 non-attainment cities**, guiding municipal investments in mechanical road sweeping and industrial emissions monitoring.
- **School Green Badge Scheme**: Implemented the "Green Schools Programme" across **6,200 primary and secondary schools**, engaging 1.5 million students in hands-on audits of school electricity, water, waste, and green cover.
